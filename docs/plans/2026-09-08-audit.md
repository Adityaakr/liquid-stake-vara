# Security audit: kVARA pool, vault, demo token

Date 2026-09-08. Scope: `programs/vara-pool`, `programs/vault`, `programs/demo-token` at
`e5c3ecf`, plus the arithmetic in `src/domain/math.ts`, `src/chain/gearAdapter.ts`,
`src/chain/varaPool.ts` and `scripts/deploy.ts`. Method: the Trail of Bits audit plugins
(audit-context-building, entry-point-analyzer, rust-review, dimensional-analysis,
property-based-testing, fp-check) run as their playbooks describe, with the Gear execution
model checked against the vara-skills references and the vendored gear 1.10 / sails 1.0.1
sources. Every candidate went through fp-check's standard verification; where a claim could
be reproduced on the local dev node it was.

Working material: `audit/ENTRY_POINTS.md` (44 state-changing commands), `audit/functions/`
(83 function analyses), `audit/DIMENSIONAL_UNITS.md` and `audit/DIMENSIONAL_ANNOTATIONS.md`
(273 dimension annotations), `audit/findings/` (raw worker findings), and the proptest suites
under `programs/*/app/tests/properties.rs`.

## Verdicts

| # | Finding | Severity | Verdict | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | ERC-4626 inflation: drive the supply to one share, fund rewards, later deposits round against their owners (pool and vault). Needs a nearly empty pool; the seed stake shields it today. Reproduced on host. | High | TRUE POSITIVE | Virtual share and asset offset on every quote (pool 1e6 planck, vault 1e3 units), priced at the base rate. Regression tests in both unit suites. |
| A2 | A token reply that never arrives (100-block default wait, no reply deposit) was handled as a refusal: redeem, claim and fee collection would restore a position that may already have been paid; deposit and reward pulls would strand tokens. Mechanism confirmed in gstd/sails sources. | Medium | TRUE POSITIVE | Reply deposit of 3B gas and a 12-hour wait on every token call; a timeout is now recorded as unconfirmed with the books assuming the money moved; `ResolveUnconfirmed(id, delivered)` for the admin. |
| A3 | Zero-gas payout to a program recipient bounces back onto the pool's balance after shares or the unbond entry are gone; the VARA was orphaned. Confirmed in core-processor precharge and reply sources. | Medium | TRUE POSITIVE | `RescueSurplus(to, amount)` for the admin, bounded to balance minus reserve minus a keep-alive buffer. Program integrators are told to hold kVARA through a user account. |
| A4 | VARA attached to any command that takes no value stayed on the program, outside the reserve, unrecoverable. Reproduced on the dev node: 1 VARA sent with `revoke_session` raised the program balance and not the reserve. | Low | TRUE POSITIVE | Every such command replies with the attached value (`no_value`), in both programs. gtests. |
| A5 | A session key stayed captured after its session expired: it could not act for itself and could not be proposed by another owner. | Low | TRUE POSITIVE | An expired session no longer binds the key; dead bindings are cleared on the next acceptance. |
| A6 | Vault `revert_redeem` used a saturating subtraction on `fees_accrued`, so a fee collected while the payout was in flight left the vault short by that fee when the payout was then refused. Also found by the property suite (F7). | Low | TRUE POSITIVE | Fees of in-flight redeems sit in `fees_pending` and only become collectable when the payout landed; the revert is exact. |
| A7 | Vault `settle_deposit` minted at least one share after the await even when the quote rounded to zero, diluting holders; property suite F6. | Low | TRUE POSITIVE | The mint is refused and the tokens are handed back (deposit reserves gas for two token calls). |
| A8 | Constructors stored `instant_fee_bps` and `unbond_period_secs` unchecked: a fee above 100% makes every exit underflow-panic; an unbond period near `u64::MAX` pins entries forever. Deployer-only. | Low | TRUE POSITIVE | Clamped in the constructor; `SetConfig` rejects unbond periods above a year. |
| A9 | Pool `stake` swept orphans before the zero-share check, so an error reply could persist a write; property suite F5. | Low | TRUE POSITIVE | Checks first, sweep after. |
| A10 | `fund_rewards` ignored pause in both programs. | Low | TRUE POSITIVE | Gated. |
| A11 | Sub-existential-deposit payouts: claimed to be refused by the runtime. Reproduced the opposite: a 0.5 VARA unstake paid 0.4985 VARA and a 0.005 VARA refund landed. Only a transfer that leaves the *recipient* below the deposit is dropped (empty account). | Medium as filed | FALSE POSITIVE as filed; residual Low | Stakes and payouts have a 1 VARA floor so a payout can never be dropped. |
| A12 | Deploy script sized the rewards tranche with an unclamped vesting window while the programs clamp it (DIM-001). | Low | TRUE POSITIVE | Clamped in the script. |
| A13 | `bufferBps` in the adapter measured reserve over assets (always above 100%) while the simulation publishes a 7.2% buffer (DIM-002). Unused by the UI. | Low | TRUE POSITIVE | Now reserve beyond what stakers own, over what they own. |
| A15 | Introduced by the A11 fix and caught by the property suite: with a 1 VARA minimum stake and a 1 VARA payout floor, a stake of exactly 1 VARA could not exit (fee or one planck of rounding put the payout under the floor). | Low | TRUE POSITIVE | Minimum stake 2 VARA; a whole position always clears the floor, asserted at the maximum fee. |
| A14 | `.max(1)` and 6-decimal assumptions in the vault (DIM-003), `Admin.burn` from any account, single-step admin transfer, unbounded per-owner unbond lists, dust funding stretching a tiny tranche by milliseconds (F4). | Info | Noted | Documented; not changed. Burn and single-step transfer are demo-token admin powers. |

The property suites ran 20,000 random sequences per program before and after the fixes (four
counterexamples in the first round became A6, A7, A9 and a doc correction; the second round found
A15 and nothing else).

What held up under attack: solvency (`reserve >= fees + unbonding + locked rewards`) on every
transition and in 20,000 random sequences per program; the vesting arithmetic (no unit or
scale mismatch anywhere, the app's projection matches the chain's linear release exactly);
the round-trip bound (a same-block in and out never profits); unbond neutrality; session
acceptance (a stranger's address cannot be bound); no unsafe, floats, hash maps or
nondeterminism; every U256 subtraction guarded, every divisor non-zero.

## Evidence notes

- A11 reproduction: `.local/subed.ts` and `.local/refund.ts` against the dev node (spec 11000,
  existential deposit 1 VARA, the same constant as mainnet).
- A4 reproduction: `.local/stuckvalue.ts`.
- A2 sources: `sails-rs-1.0.1/src/client/gstd_env.rs` (reply deposit defaults to 0, per-call
  `wait_up_to`), `gstd-1.10.0/src/config.rs` (100-block default), `gstd-1.10.0/src/common/errors.rs`
  (`Error::Timeout`, `timed_out()`).
- A3 sources: core-processor `precharge.rs` and `processing.rs` (value returned with the system
  error reply), as cited in `audit/findings/gear-2.md`.

## Residual risks and recommendations

- A program recipient of a pool payout still loses it until the admin rescues the surplus;
  integrators should route through a user account. A future version could carry a small gas
  budget on payouts and reconcile through `handle_reply`.
- Unconfirmed vault calls need an operator to look at the token's state and resolve them; the
  12-hour wait makes them rare, not impossible. Watch `Unconfirmed()`.
- `transfer_admin` is single-step in all three programs; a two-step handover is worth adding
  before the admin key is anything but a hardware wallet.
- The demo token's admin can mint without limit and burn from any account. It is a demo asset;
  the vault does not depend on either power.
